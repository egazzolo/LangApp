# Voice accents — current launch policy

All plans request neutral General American pronunciation for English voice notes. Regional accent selection is disabled pending audio validation. Character origin and legacy accent preferences do not override this policy. Spanish retains its selected Spanish pronunciation variant.

The localized notice is displayed in Create Ferson (for English) and Settings. App updates are needed for the notice; server changes apply only to newly generated audio. Existing audio is unchanged.

A future British option should target modern RP / Standard Southern British English and be auditioned before release. Prompt compliance is not proof of audible accent quality.

Ferson

Practice English with an AI conversation partner.

Meet Ferson: fake + person, a fluent English-speaking AI partner you can practice with whenever you have time.

Create your own Ferson and choose a personality and interests that give you something to talk about. Talk about your day, work, plans or things you enjoy. Send text and voice messages, listen to replies and keep the conversation going.

Stuck for words? What you could say suggests a response and helps you understand useful expressions. Try saying it in your own words.

Ask Tutor to review your messages, explain mistakes and show better ways to express yourself. Use what you learn in your next conversation.

Fersons are fictional AI characters, not real people.
